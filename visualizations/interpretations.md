# Visualisation interpretations

## 01_value_by_molecule

Lenalidomide and oncology-bundle tenders (Axitinib via LIS 2207) dominate headline NOK, but these are framework ceilings — not addressable pack revenue. Anagrelide's published ~10 MNOK competition is far smaller but may be fully contestable.

## 02_notice_lifecycle

Lenalidomide and Paliperidone show multiple notice types (prior information, competition, intent), reflecting the assessment's lifecycle point. Axitinib appears mainly inside bundled oncology tenders — a different buying route with less standalone visibility.

## 03_strength_distribution

Everolimus and Lenalidomide span many strengths — typical for immunosuppressant and myeloma portfolios. Fewer Axitinib strengths but oncology bundle procurement means winning requires competing across a wide co-formulary, not a single SKU.

## 04_detection_methods

Name matching alone would miss Axitinib (ATC-only in attachments) and supplemental Paliperidone lines (N05AX13). Combining name, LIS ref, and ATC code matching is necessary for Norwegian LIS data.

## 05_publication_timing

Active 2025–2026 competitions (Everolimus 2632a, Paliperidone 2601c) sit in the bid window now, while Lenalidomide's main LIS 2234 cycle is older — suggesting near-term bid effort should focus on live tenders, not historic high-value frameworks.
